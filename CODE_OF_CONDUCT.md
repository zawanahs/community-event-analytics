# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be safe, respectful, and constructive.

Contributors must communicate with empathy, welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels, respect privacy, and focus criticism on ideas and implementation rather than individuals. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method listed on the repository. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, fairly, and without retaliation.
